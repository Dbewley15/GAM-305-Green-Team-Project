Enemy tracking capability

Game Concept and Brainstorming
Theme/Concept: First-Person Shooter
The Green Team will develop a single first-person level centered around exploration, combat, and completing objectives. 
Players will navigate the environment, encounter enemies, collect pickups, and use elevators and jump pads to reach different areas of the level. 
The enemy tracking capability will help the player locate or monitor enemies during gameplay.
The team will collaborate on the final setting, visual style, enemy designs, objectives, level layout, and implementation of the four selected gameplay elements. 
The goal is to create a playable first-person experience that demonstrates the required scenario and additional elements while remaining achievable within the course timeline.

Development Timeline

Establish the team repository and repository access.
Establish team roles.
Select the project scenario.
Select four additional gameplay elements.

Begin creating the first-person level and basic environment.

Create initial enemy and pickup functionality.
Establish the basic UI and level flow.
Test individual features as they are completed.

Alpha Stage
The goal for the Alpha submission is to have the core gameplay and major systems implemented in a playable state. 
The first-person level should have its basic layout, player movement, enemies, pickups, elevators, jump pads, and initial enemy tracking functionality.

Basic UI elements and gameplay objectives should also be in place. The team will use the Alpha stage to identify bugs, missing features, and systems that need additional development.
Progress will be tracked through the team's task list, Discord updates, testing, and repository commits.

Beta Stage
The goal for the Beta submission is to have all major gameplay elements implemented and functioning together in a mostly complete level. 
The team will focus on polishing the environment, UI, gameplay mechanics, enemy behavior, pickups, elevators, jump pads, and enemy tracking system.
During Beta, the team will concentrate on testing, bug fixing, balancing, and improving the overall player experience. 
Progress will be tracked through completed task items, testing results, Discord updates, and repository commits.

Final Development
Complete remaining gameplay features.
Fix remaining bugs and technical issues.
Polish the level environment and UI.
Conduct final playtesting.
Make necessary adjustments based on testing.
Verify that all required scenario and additional elements are functional.
Prepare the final project for submission.

Team Communication
Primary Communication Method: Discord
Meeting/Communication Frequency: Sunday through Tuesday
The team will use the Green Team Discord thread and voice channel for project communication, planning, questions, and development coordination. 
Team members are expected to check Discord regularly during the scheduled communication days and provide updates when completing assigned tasks or encountering problems.
Discord will also be used to discuss problems, share progress, coordinate development tasks, and make decisions that affect the project.

Task Assignment and Progress Reporting
The team will use a shared task list to assign and track individual responsibilities. Each task will include the assigned team member, a description of the task, and its current status.
Tasks will be organized into the following categories:
Not Started
In Progress
Testing
Completed
Team members will report their progress through Discord and update the task list as work is completed. 
If a team member encounters a problem or is unable to complete a task, they will communicate the issue to the team so responsibilities or deadlines can be adjusted as needed.
Repository commits will also be used to track development progress and provide a record of completed work.

Alpha and Beta Progress Tracking
The team will use the task list, Discord communication, repository commits, and playtesting to monitor project progress.
For the Alpha stage, progress will be measured by whether the core gameplay systems and required gameplay elements are implemented and playable.
For the Beta stage, progress will be measured by whether all major systems are working together, the level is mostly complete, and the project has moved into testing, bug fixing, and polish.
This process will allow the team to identify unfinished work early and make adjustments before the final submission.

Team Quality Assurance and Testing Plan
Quality Assurance Communication and Collaboration
The Green Team will use Discord as the primary means of communication for quality assurance and testing. 
Team members will use the team's Discord thread and voice channel to discuss test results, report bugs, review changes, and determine which issues need to be addressed.
The team will collaboratively develop and maintain the test plan in the GitHub repository. 
Team members will contribute test cases based on their areas of responsibility and review testing results with the rest of the team. 
The shared test plan will be updated whenever changes are made to the game or design document.
The team will use the shared task list, Discord, and GitHub repository to communicate testing responsibilities and record progress.

Testing Schedule
Testing will take place throughout the development cycle instead of waiting until the end of the project.

Play Test - Preproduction Stage
During preproduction and early development, the team will conduct play tests of individual mechanics and early level builds.
The purpose of the Play Test stage is to determine whether the basic gameplay systems are working as intended and whether the level design supports the planned player experience.
The team will test:
Player movement and controls
Basic level navigation
Player interaction with the environment
Enemy behavior
Pickups
Elevators
Jump pads
Enemy tracking
Basic UI
Level objectives

Issues discovered during Play Testing will be reported and assigned before the affected feature moves into the next development stage.

Demo - Before Marketing Demo
Before the project is presented as a demonstration, the team will conduct a full game playthrough using the current build.
The Demo test will verify that the major gameplay systems work together and that a player can complete the intended level without encountering major problems.
The team will check:
Complete level progression
Player movement and controls
Enemy encounters
Pickup functionality
Elevator functionality
Jump pad functionality
Enemy tracking functionality
UI and objectives
Level transitions
Collision and physics
Audio and visual elements
Game performance
Major bugs and gameplay blockers

Any critical problems discovered during the Demo test will be prioritized before the project is demonstrated.
Code Release - Test Plan Verification
Before a code release or final build is submitted, the team will perform a final test using the current version of the test plan.
The team will verify that previously reported bugs have been fixed and that fixes have not created new problems in other parts of the game.
The Code Release test will include:
Running all required test cases.
Confirming that required gameplay elements are functional.
Rechecking previously failed test cases.
Testing recently changed features.
Checking for new bugs caused by changes.
Confirming that major gameplay systems work together.
Confirming that the current build matches the design requirements.
Recording final Pass/Fail results.

A code release will not be considered ready until critical failures have been addressed or documented for the team.

Updating the Test Plan
The test plan will be updated whenever changes are made to the game, gameplay mechanics, level design, assets, UI, or design document.
When a new feature is added, the team will determine whether a new test case is needed. 
If an existing feature is changed, the related test case will be reviewed and updated to reflect the new expected behavior.
The team will review the test plan during major development milestones, including Alpha, Beta, Demo, and Code Release. 
This will help make sure that the testing requirements remain consistent with the current version of the game.
Changes to the test plan will be communicated through Discord and recorded in the GitHub repository so the team can see what was changed and why.

Bug Reporting
Bugs will be reported through the team's shared task list and discussed through Discord.
Each bug report should include:
Bug title
Date reported
Team member who reported the bug
Description of the problem
Steps to reproduce the problem
Expected result
Actual result
Severity or priority
Game/build version
Assigned team member
Current status

Screenshots or video clips may also be included when they help explain the problem.
Critical bugs that prevent the player from progressing or prevent a major game system from functioning will be communicated to the team immediately.

Bug Tracking and Changes Over Time
The team will track bugs through the shared task list and GitHub repository. Each reported bug will receive a status so the team can follow it from discovery through resolution.

Bug statuses will include:
Reported - The problem has been identified but has not been reviewed.
Confirmed - The team has verified that the problem exists.
In Progress - A team member is working on a solution.
Testing - A potential fix has been completed and needs to be tested.
Resolved - The fix has been tested and the original problem no longer occurs.
Closed - The team has confirmed that the issue is resolved.
Deferred - The team has decided that the issue will be addressed later because of its priority or available development time.

When a bug is fixed, the team member responsible for the fix will update the task and provide information about the change. 
The team will then retest the affected feature and any related features that could have been affected by the change.
GitHub commits will provide an additional record of development changes. The team will use these records to help identify when changes were made and 
determine whether a new problem may have been introduced by a recent update.
This process will allow the team to maintain a clear record of bugs, fixes, testing results, and changes throughout the development cycle.
