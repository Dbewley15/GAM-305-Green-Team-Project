Selected Scenario
The Green Team has selected the First-Person scenario for the project.

Additional Elements
The team's four additional elements are:
•	Elevators
•	Jump pads
•	Pickups
•	Enemy tracking capability
These elements will be incorporated into the first-person level and will support the overall gameplay experience.

Game Concept and Brainstorming
Theme/Concept: First-Person Shooter
The Green Team will develop a single first-person level centered around exploration, combat, and completing objectives. Players will navigate the environment, encounter enemies, collect pickups, and use elevators and jump pads to reach different areas of the level. The enemy tracking capability will help the player locate or monitor enemies during gameplay.
The team will collaborate on the final setting, visual style, enemy designs, objectives, level layout, and implementation of the four selected gameplay elements. The goal is to create a playable first-person experience that demonstrates the required scenario and additional elements while remaining achievable within the course timeline.

Development Timeline
Initial Planning / Module Two
•	Establish the team repository and repository access.
•	Establish team roles.
•	Select the project scenario.
•	Select four additional gameplay elements.
•	Establish the game's theme and overall design.
•	Determine communication and task-management procedures.
Pre-Alpha Development
•	Begin creating the first-person level and basic environment.
•	Implement player movement and basic FPS mechanics.
•	Begin creating the selected gameplay elements.
•	Create initial enemy and pickup functionality.
•	Establish the basic UI and level flow.
•	Test individual features as they are completed.
Alpha Stage
The goal for the Alpha submission is to have the core gameplay and major systems implemented in a playable state. The first-person level should have its basic layout, player movement, enemies, pickups, elevators, jump pads, and initial enemy tracking functionality.
Basic UI elements and gameplay objectives should also be in place. The team will use the Alpha stage to identify bugs, missing features, and systems that need additional development.
Progress will be tracked through the team's task list, Discord updates, testing, and repository commits.
Beta Stage
The goal for the Beta submission is to have all major gameplay elements implemented and functioning together in a mostly complete level. The team will focus on polishing the environment, UI, gameplay mechanics, enemy behavior, pickups, elevators, jump pads, and enemy tracking system.
During Beta, the team will concentrate on testing, bug fixing, balancing, and improving the overall player experience. Progress will be tracked through completed task items, testing results, Discord updates, and repository commits.
Final Development
•	Complete remaining gameplay features.
•	Fix remaining bugs and technical issues.
•	Polish the level environment and UI.
•	Conduct final playtesting.
•	Make necessary adjustments based on testing.
•	Verify that all required scenario and additional elements are functional.
•	Prepare the final project for submission.

Team Communication
Primary Communication Method: Discord
Meeting/Communication Frequency: Sunday through Tuesday
The team will use the Green Team Discord thread and voice channel for project communication, planning, questions, and development coordination. Team members are expected to check Discord regularly during the scheduled communication days and provide updates when completing assigned tasks or encountering problems.
Discord will also be used to discuss problems, share progress, coordinate development tasks, and make decisions that affect the project.

Task Assignment and Progress Reporting
The team will use a shared task list to assign and track individual responsibilities. Each task will include the assigned team member, a description of the task, and its current status.
Tasks will be organized into the following categories:
•	Not Started
•	In Progress
•	Testing
•	Completed
Team members will report their progress through Discord and update the task list as work is completed. If a team member encounters a problem or is unable to complete a task, they will communicate the issue to the team so responsibilities or deadlines can be adjusted as needed.
Repository commits will also be used to track development progress and provide a record of completed work.

Alpha and Beta Progress Tracking
The team will use the task list, Discord communication, repository commits, and playtesting to monitor project progress.
For the Alpha stage, progress will be measured by whether the core gameplay systems and required gameplay elements are implemented and playable.
For the Beta stage, progress will be measured by whether all major systems are working together, the level is mostly complete, and the project has moved into testing, bug fixing, and polish.
This process will allow the team to identify unfinished work early and make adjustments before the final submission.
